# Birca Distillery — Sales Website Proposal

A lightweight website concept for a Latvian craft producer, built to demonstrate how a clear digital presence could support product discovery, visits, and direct enquiries.

[Open the demo](https://romanmamrukov.github.io/bircaWebDemo/)

## Project status

**Status:** independent proposal demo.

This is not the official Birca website and must not be presented as a completed client engagement. Company information was assembled from the supplied flyer and public business presence for proposal purposes; it requires owner verification before production use.

## Design objective

The demo replaces a generic brochure structure with a direct commercial sequence:

1. establish product and regional identity;
2. explain what is produced and why it is distinctive;
3. make products and tasting context easy to understand;
4. provide clear visit and contact paths;
5. support future multilingual and QR-led campaigns.

## Included

- responsive HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript;
- clear product and provenance hierarchy;
- direct enquiry and location paths;
- crawlable metadata, `robots.txt`, and `sitemap.xml`;
- GitHub Pages-compatible deployment;
- user-supplied reference flyer assets;
- self-created placeholder artwork for content still awaiting photography.

## Run locally

```bash
python -m http.server 8080
```

Open `http://localhost:8080`.

## Production checklist

Before this concept becomes an official website:

- confirm the legal business name, address, phone, email, and opening information;
- replace placeholders with licensed, production-quality photography;
- approve LV / RU / EN copy with the owner;
- connect enquiries to an owned inbox or CRM;
- add privacy, cookie, and alcohol-related legal content where applicable;
- configure analytics and campaign parameters only after consent requirements are defined;
- deploy through an account and domain controlled by the business.
